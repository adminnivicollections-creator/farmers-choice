## What the app does not collect

- **No account.** The app has no sign-in, so it does not ask for your name or
  phone number.
- **No contacts, photos, files, or messages.**

## Location

The app asks for your location only while you use it. It is never used in the
background.

| Used for | What leaves your phone | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| Weather and rain forecast on Home | Your position rounded to about 1 km | [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) |
| Showing your village or town name | Your position | Google, through your phone's built-in place-name service |
| Choosing the paddy zone in the fertilizer calculator | Nothing extra; uses the same place name | — |
| Measuring land by walking or drawing | Nothing; points stay on your phone | — |

Our own server does not receive or store your location.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read QR codes. Pictures are not saved or sent
anywhere. The text inside a scanned QR code is sent to our server to check
whether it is one of ours. With it we store a random ID created by the app when
it was installed (it is not linked to your name or phone number), the result,
and the time. We keep these records to spot copied product labels.

## Saved land measurements and scan history

Saved land measurements and your recent scans list are stored only on your
phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are **not personalised**. AdMob may
still collect device information such as the advertising ID to show and count
ads, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

- Turn off location or camera access at any time in your phone's settings. The
  app keeps working without them; only the features that need them stop.
- To ask us to delete scan records linked to your install ID, write to
  [email] with the ID shown in [where the app shows it, if you add this].

## Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes.
