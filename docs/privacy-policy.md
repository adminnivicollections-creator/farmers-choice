# Farmer's Choice — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 6 November 2026._

Farmer's Choice ("the app") is published by S Harsha Vardhan Reddy, Macherla,
Andhra Pradesh, India. Contact: [vanaisolution@gmail.com](mailto:vanaisolution@gmail.com).

Farmer's Choice is an independent app. It is not affiliated with ANGRAU, ICAR
or any government body.

## In short

- No account, no sign-in. The app never asks for your name, phone number or
  email.
- Only an **approximate** location (about 1 km) ever leaves your phone, and only
  to show weather and your village or town name.
- Your saved fields and scan history stay on your phone.
- Ads are shown by Google AdMob and are **not personalised**.

## Location

The app asks for your location only while you use it, never in the background.

| Used for | What leaves your phone | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| Weather and rain alerts | Your position rounded to about 1 km | Our server, which asks [OpenWeather](https://openweathermap.org) and keeps the answer in memory for 10 minutes. If our server is unavailable, the app asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) directly |
| Your village or town name | Your position rounded to about 1 km | Google, through your phone's built-in place-name service |
| Choosing the paddy zone in the fertilizer calculator | Nothing extra; uses the same place name | — |
| Measuring land by walking or drawing | Nothing; the points stay on your phone | — |

Our server does not store your location.

## Camera and QR codes

The camera is used only to read QR codes. Pictures are not saved or sent
anywhere, and the content of a scanned code is read on your phone — it is not
sent to our server. If the code is a website, the app opens it in your browser;
payment, phone and map codes open only when you tap.

## On your phone only

Saved land measurements, your recent scans and your language choice are stored
only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are not personalised. To show and
count ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device information such as
the advertising ID, IP address and app interactions, as described in
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

## Our server

Our server keeps the usual short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses)
needed to run it and to limit abuse. We do not sell data or share it with
anyone except the services named above.

## Children

The app is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

You can turn off location or camera access at any time in your phone's
settings. The app keeps working; only the features that need them stop.

We will update this page and its effective date when the policy changes.
