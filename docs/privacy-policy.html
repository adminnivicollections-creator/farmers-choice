Farmer's Choice is an independent app. It is not affiliated with ANGRAU, ICAR or any government body.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in. The app never asks for your name, phone number or email.
  • Only an approximate location (about 1 km) ever leaves your phone, and only to show weather and your village or town name.
  • Your saved fields and scan history stay on your phone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob and are not personalised.

Location

The app asks for your location only while you use it, never in the background.

Camera and QR codes

The camera is used only to read QR codes. Pictures are not saved or sent anywhere, and the content of a scanned code is read on your phone — it is not sent to our server. If the code is a website, the app opens it in your browser; payment, phone and map codes open only when you tap.

On your phone only

Saved land measurements, your recent scans and your language choice are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are not personalised. To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app interactions, as described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Our server

Our server keeps the usual short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) needed to run it and to limit abuse. We do not sell data or share it with anyone except the services named above.

Children

The app is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can turn off location or camera access at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working; only the features that need them stop.
